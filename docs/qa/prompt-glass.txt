High-fidelity mobile UI design mockup, tall portrait 9:16 aspect, for a Persian RTL astrology web app called "Zayche" (زایچه).

Aesthetic: premium night-sky. Deep navy background (#0d1128) with a soft purple aurora glow in the top corner, glassmorphism cards with hairline light borders and subtle translucency, single gold accent color (#f5c518). Vazirmatn-style Persian typography, elegant spacing, modern SaaS quality like Linear or Stripe dark mode.

Layout top to bottom:
1. Floating pill-shaped appbar: gold brand mark "زایچه" on the right, small outlined gold credit pill "۷ اعتبار" on the left.
2. Large Persian greeting headline "سلام مهدی" with muted subtitle line under it.
3. Row of three KPI stat chips in one segmented card: large gold numerals ۷ / ۳ / ۲ with tiny gray labels (اعتبار، گزارش فعال، گذر پیش‌رو).
4. Wide featured glass card with a moon icon tile on the right: title «آسمان امروز تو», subtitle «ماه در خانهٔ دهم — روزِ دیده‌شدن», small left arrow.
5. 2x2 grid of navigation cards, each with a small rounded icon tile, bold title and muted caption: گزارش‌های من، سازگاری، افزودن اعتبار، تنظیمات.
6. Fixed dark bottom navigation bar with five items, the center item raised inside a glowing gold circle labeled چارت.

Realistic Farsi content everywhere, crisp rendering, no watermark, no lorem ipsum.
